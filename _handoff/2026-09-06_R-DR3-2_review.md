## VERDICT

**FIX_REQUIRED — 7 BLOCKING (B1–B7), 10 NON-BLOCKING (N1–N10).**

The report's **headline conclusion is correct and worth keeping**: 9 dB does not reduce the amplifier's worst-case
output, because the binding ceiling is the 5 V rail, not the gain; and no upper bound on supply current is derivable
at any gain. Its gain-table/`PinMap.hpp` agreement check, its three M-PEAK relabels (3.2 W typ, ILIM typ, IDD max at
+25 °C only) and its pre-solder GAIN checklist are sound and are the pass's real value.

But the quantitative core is broken in the unsafe direction. **The per-engine-state figures are labelled "≥" (lower
bounds) and are ~7.4× too high**, because every input was chosen at the upper edge — including a sine crest factor
the report declared "NOT DERIVABLE" and which is in fact exactly derivable from the firmware constants. Those numbers
are then written onto BG-03 S4 as a **diagnostic band** ("a reading below the band means the amp was not rendering"),
which would tell an operator that a correctly working amplifier is broken. Separately, **§2.4's "self-consistency
check" is arithmetically wrong by an order of magnitude**, and **every one of §6's "verbatim" anchor quotes is stale**
— taken from `offline/o6-v3-brief`, not from the r4/dr3-apply state the cards are actually in.

### B2 — The per-engine-state figures are labelled "≥" but are upper-bound-shaped, and are ~7.4× too high

**Claim.** §3.1's table and §6.1/§6.2/§6.3's card text: "**≥ 26 mA**" (Cranking @80), "**≥ 43 mA**" (Running/idle
@80), "**≥ 346 mA**" (Running/full @80, typ) / "**≥ 397 mA**" (gain max), "**≥ 540 / 620 mA**" (volume 100).

**Re-derivation.** I reproduced the report's own arithmetic exactly (56/72/137/204/255 composed bytes, scale
`byte/255 × 30000/32768`, ×3.5892 V, ÷4 Ω, ÷5 V — all of it checks). The arithmetic is right; **the direction is
wrong at every input**:

| Input | Report's choice | Direction for a **lower** bound | Correct choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gain | **9.6 dB MAX** ("per M-PEAK") | raises the figure | **8.4 dB MIN** (`max98357a.txt`:264) |
| Digital peak | **30000** design cap | raises it | actual profile peak (V10 24 600, `EngineSynth.hpp`:87-90) |
| Crest factor | **√2** (a sine) | raises it — the report itself says the real one is higher (§3.1 A5) | the real one |
| Output rail | ideal **3.5355 V RMS** | raises it | ≈ 3.15 V RMS (see B4) |

The report is explicit that its P_out table is an **upper** bound (§3.1: "The table is therefore an **upper bound on
output power**") and then divides it by VDD and writes "≥". `I ≥ P_out/VDD` needs a **lower** bound on P_out. The
sign is simply wrong. Applying M-PEAK's "use the max" to a lower-bound quantity is a misreading of DR2-2: M-PEAK
picks the band edge that is conservative **for the direction the figure is used in**, and for a floor that is the
minimum.

**Magnitude.** The crest factor is not "NOT DERIVABLE" (§8 item 8). For a sum of sines at **distinct** harmonics the
RMS is exactly `sqrt(Σaᵢ²/2)` and is **phase-independent**, so it follows directly from the firmware constants:

- V10 (`EngineSynth.hpp`:87-90): partials {2200, 4400, 5600, 4000, 2600, 1400}, whine 2800 at 3.0× the fundamental
  (`whinePitchEighths = 24`, `:93`) — i.e. **coherent with partial index 2**, so fold it in at worst-case phase:
  {2200, 4400, **8400**, 4000, 2600, 1400}; noise uniform ±1600 at redline (`EngineSynth.cpp`:127) → RMS = 1600/√3.
  **Total RMS ≤ 7 784** of 32768 (crest factor ≥ 3.16 against the 24 600 coherent peak, ≥ 3.85 against 30 000).
- V6 (`SynthProfiles.hpp`:55-69, whine 4200 at 5.0× folded onto partial index 4): **RMS ≤ 7 622**. Same conclusion.

This is itself an **upper** bound (worst-case phase, redline noise, whine fully open). The report assumed
30000/√2 = 21 213. Ratio 0.367 in voltage, **0.135 in power → the table overstates output power by ≈ 7.4×.**

Correct sine-**equivalent** figures at **gain typ 9 dB**, VDD 5.0 V, 4 Ω, using the RMS above and
`output dBV = dBFS + 2.1 + gain` (`:2036-2038`), 0 dBFS = a full-scale sine (RMS 23 170):

| State | byte | dBFS | V RMS | P (4 Ω) | P/VDD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cranking @80 | 56 | −22.6 | 0.265 | 0.018 W | **3.5 mA** (report: 26) |
| Running/idle @80 | 72 | −20.5 | 0.340 | 0.029 W | **5.8 mA** (report: 43) |
| Running/50 % @80 | 137 | −14.9 | 0.648 | 0.105 W | **21 mA** (report: 156) |
| Running/full @80 | 204 | −11.4 | 0.965 | 0.233 W | **47 mA** (report: 346) |
| Running/full @100 | 255 | −9.5 | 1.206 | 0.364 W | **73 mA** (report: 540) |

The Cranking figure lands **below the part's own quiescent current** (2.75 mA typ / 3.35 mA max, `:200`). That is the
proof that the report's band is unusable: at the acceptance state the audio-derived component of the amp's supply
current is of the same order as its quiescent draw, and the report's card text says it is 346 mA.

**Required change.** Delete every "≥ … mA" per-state figure from §6.1, §6.2 and §6.3. If any per-state figure is
carried at all, carry it in this exact form and nowhere else:

> **Sine-equivalent ESTIMATE, NOT a bound and NOT M-PEAK-clean** (gain **typ** 9 dB, VDD 5.0 V, 4 Ω, synth RMS
> computed from the firmware partial/noise/whine constants, `EngineSynth.hpp`:87-90, `EngineSynth.cpp`:127-137,
> `SynthProfiles.hpp`:55-69): the audio-derived component of the amp's supply current is of order **5 mA** at
> Cranking / Running-idle at `sound.volume` 80 and of order **50 mA** at Running + full throttle at 80 — i.e. the
> same order as the part's own quiescent current. **This is an estimate, it is not a floor, it must never be entered
> in a `P(N)` cell, a `Σ P(k)` term or a rail allowance, and it is not a PASS criterion for any reading.**

### B3 — §6.3's "sanity-check band" is a false diagnostic criterion on a safety card

**Claim.** §6.3's proposed `P(N)` cell: "**Sanity-check band** … **Cranking ≥ 26 mA · Running at idle throttle ≥
43 mA** at `sound.volume` 80 … **A reading below the band means the amp was not rendering, not that the amp is
efficient.**"

**Evidence.** By B2 the true audio-derived floor at those two states is ≈ **3.5 mA** and ≈ **5.8 mA**. A correctly
working, actually-rendering amplifier at S4 will read roughly an order of magnitude below the band. The sentence
therefore instructs the operator to conclude *the amp was not rendering* from evidence that says nothing of the kind
— a fabricated fault on a first-power card, at the exact substep where the alternative diagnosis (a wiring or I²S
fault) invites re-work on a partly energised bench.

It is also unsafe in the other direction: "a reading below the band means X" makes "a reading inside the band" read
as confirmation, i.e. a de-facto **PASS criterion** on a row whose `P(4)` is BLOCKED. BG-03 S4 must not acquire a
pass criterion while its bound does not exist.

**Required change.** Delete the band entirely. Replace with:

> **There is no derivable expected value for this reading.** The amp's audio-derived supply current at the states
> this staircase can reach is of the same order as its own quiescent current (3.35 mA max at +25 °C, `:200`), so a
> low reading is **not** evidence that the amp was silent and a higher one is **not** a pass. The only admissible
> interpretation of this reading is the one already required below: record the engine state and `sound.volume`, and
> if the amp was silent this row stays THRESHOLD MISSING.

### B4 — §2.4's "self-consistency check" is wrong by an order of magnitude, and the wrong number is in §6.1's card text

**Claim.** §2.4 and §6.1: "3.2 W is **1.024×** [3.125 W] — 0.10 dB — **exactly what a 10 %-THD clipped waveform adds
over a clean sine at the same peak**. The published 12 dB number is the rail, not the gain, confirming A3 against the
manufacturer's own data."

**Re-derivation.** For a symmetrically hard-clipped sine, the power relative to the maximum unclipped sine at the same
peak is a computable function of the clipping ratio. I solved it numerically:

| THD | clip ratio m | P / P(max clean sine) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 % | 0.972 | **1.048× (+0.20 dB)** |
| 10 % | 0.779 | **1.288× (+1.10 dB)** |

A 10 %-THD clipped sine adds **+1.10 dB**, not +0.10 dB. The report's "exactly" is a coincidence of two unrelated
numbers, and the inference drawn from it is invalid.

**What the datasheet's own rows actually say.** Inverting the clipping factor on all four `POUT` rows (`:234-247`)
recovers the amplifier's real output swing, and it is strikingly self-consistent:

| Row | Published | Clean-sine equivalent | V RMS | V peak | implied series drop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 Ω, 10 % THD | 3.2 W | 2.485 W | 3.153 | 4.459 | 0.49 Ω |
| 4 Ω, 1 % THD | 2.5 W | 2.387 W | 3.090 | 4.370 | 0.58 Ω |
| 8 Ω, 10 % THD | 1.8 W | 1.398 W | 3.344 | 4.729 | 0.46 Ω |
| 8 Ω, 1 % THD | 1.4 W | 1.337 W | 3.270 | 4.623 | 0.65 Ω |

The two 10 %-THD rows (the ones actually at clipping onset) agree on ≈ **0.46–0.49 Ω** of total series resistance
(H-bridge `RDS(on)` — for which the datasheet publishes **no** numeric spec, only the qualitative mention at `:2003`
— plus the test inductor's DCR). Consequences the report gets wrong:

1. **The real clean-sine ceiling into 4 Ω is ≈ 2.49 W (3.15 V RMS), not 3.125 W**; into 8 Ω ≈ 1.40 W, not 1.5625 W.
   The ideal-rail figure **overstates** the achievable clean-sine output by ~25 %. As a *ceiling* that is the safe
   direction, so 3.125 W may be carried — but it must be labelled "ideal-rail, ~25 % above what the datasheet's own
   `POUT` rows imply", not "validated by the datasheet".
2. **"Only at the 8.4 dB guaranteed minimum is it gain-limited" is false.** Full scale at 8.4 dB is 3.350 V RMS,
   which is **above** the ≈ 3.15 V RMS real clipping level. The part is **supply-limited at every one of the three
   gain band edges**, not two of three. (This strengthens the headline conclusion.)
3. The quoted margins — "0.13 dB over rail" (typ), "0.73 dB" (max), "0.47 dB under" (min) — are all wrong. The real
   figures are ≈ +1.1 dB, +1.7 dB and +0.55 dB **over** the achievable swing.
4. §3.1's "**Nothing in the shipped configuration clips**" is unsupported: at gain typ, volume 100 and the 30 000 peak
   the predicted peak output is 4.65 V, above the ≈ 4.46 V the datasheet's own rows imply is reachable into 4 Ω. The
   synth's peaks clip at volume 100. (Not in §6 text — see N2 — but it must not stay in the report body either.)

**Required change.** Delete the parenthetical from §6.1 and replace it with:

> (The 12 dB ceiling is confirmed directly by the datasheet's own published figure — **3.2 W into 4 Ω + 33 µH at
> 10 % THD+N, gain 12 dB, VDD 5 V**, `:234-236` — which is a rail-set number, not a gain-set one: the gain equation
> alone would predict 6.43 W at 12 dB. The ideal-rail 3.125 W carried here is **~25 % above** the clean-sine output
> the same rows imply is actually reachable (≈ 2.49 W into 4 Ω, once the ≈ 0.5 Ω of H-bridge and test-inductor series
> resistance implied by all four `POUT` rows is accounted for), so it is conservative as a ceiling and must not be
> read as an achievable output.)

And in §2/§6.1/§6.3 replace "supply-limited by 0.13 dB / 0.73 dB" with "supply-limited at **all three** gain band
edges (8.4 / 9 / 9.6 dB)".

### B5 — §6.5 / §4.2-4: the sizing-sum substitution is mis-cited *and* invents a design load

**Claim.** §4.2 item 4 and §6.5: "**Swap the amp term in T11's sizing sum** from 640 mA to 1600 mA"; `first_power_current_limits.md`
T11 (:106) "and the O-6 addendum's §6.4 arithmetic use 640 mA … in `1800 + 188 + 640 = 2628 mA` (52.56 %)".

**(a) Bad cite — BLOCKING on its own.** I read T11 (`…first_power_current_limits.md`:106) in full. It carries the
640 mA term but **contains no sizing sum at all** — no `1800 + 188 + 640`, no 2628, no percentage. `grep` across the
r4 worktree puts that arithmetic in exactly two places, both **owner-facing state**:
`W17_OWNER_ACTIONS.md`:**64** (the DR2-13 ruling's rationale) and `CURRENT_STATUS.md`:**21** and **:25**. An applier
following §6.5 would edit the wrong row and leave the two live copies untouched — or, worse, would be led to edit
owner-facing ruling text on a worker's flag.

**(b) Wrong in substance.** The arithmetic checks (3588 mA = 71.76 %, 3960 mA = 79.20 %, and the 620 mA variant
2608 mA = 52.16 % / 2980 mA = 59.60 % — all verified). The *substitution* does not:

- **±1.6 A is not a load.** It is an Absolute Maximum stress rating whose own preamble says functional operation at
  it "is not implied" (`:182-184`). A sizing sum adds up what the rail must **supply**; entering a damage threshold
  as a design load manufactures a term.
- **It is physically unreachable.** By B4 the amp's output into 4 Ω is capped at ≈ 2.5 W by the rail; even at an
  implausibly poor 60 % efficiency that is ≈ 0.83 A of supply current, and at the datasheet's typ 92 % ≈ 0.54 A. The
  substitution would allocate ~0.8 A of Rail A to a current the part cannot draw into its own speaker, and would move
  the headline utilisation from 53 % to 72 % on that fiction — the opposite of the honesty the change claims.
- **DR2-2 does not license it.** M-PEAK is about manufacturer-specified maximum **operating/rated** values used as
  added-load allowances — that is how L11 (Wi-Fi 1800 mA rated max supply current) and L12 (servo stall) are used. An
  absolute maximum rating is a different category. Calling it "M-PEAK-clean" for this purpose is a category error, and
  "0 % invented margin" forbids inserting a number the part will never draw.
- R-ADD already relabelled this sum as a **mixed-direction sizing sum, not a floor**. The correct fix is to make the
  amp term honest, not to make it big.

**Required change.** Withdraw the recommendation. If §6.5 is kept at all, it must read:

> **FLAG, not a proposal.** The Rail-A sizing sum at `W17_OWNER_ACTIONS.md`:64 and `CURRENT_STATUS.md`:21,:25 (**not**
> T11, which carries the 640 mA term but no sum) uses **≥ 640 mA** — a lower bound at the 12 dB spec point, itself
> resting on a **typ** (`:2542-2543`) — where a sizing sum needs an upper-bound-shaped term. The defensible fix is to
> carry the amplifier term as **UNBOUNDED / BLOCKED** and to state that the sum therefore cannot express Rail-A
> headroom, **not** to substitute the ±1.6 A absolute maximum, which is a stress rating the part cannot draw into a
> 4 Ω or 8 Ω load at 5 V and which would invent a ~0.8 A design load. Because these two files are owner-facing state
> attached to the DR2-13 ruling, any change to the printed percentages is an **owner adjudication**, not a worker fix.

### B6 — "±1.6 A is the amplifier branch's never-exceed" is missing the sentence that stops it being dialled in

**Claim.** §6.1 puts ±1.6 A in L13's **Value** column and §6.2/§6.3 put it in the **never-exceed** column, described
as "**this is the amplifier branch's never-exceed**", "M-PEAK-clean".

**Evidence.** The text is exactly as quoted (`:171`, "Continuous Current In/Out of V_DD/GND/OUT_ … ±1.6 A") and the
report correctly repeats the stress-rating caveat. Three problems remain:

1. **It provides no protection as a limit setting.** The amp cannot draw more than ~0.8 A into its own load (B5).
   A 1.6 A trip point on the amp branch would sit ~2× above any fault the branch can produce short of a dead short —
   and it silently contradicts T11's ratified policy, "lowest defensible limit per substep".
2. **L13's Value column is where allowances are read from.** T11 states "All figures below are 5 V rail-side
   added-load allowances". Putting 1600 mA there is precisely the pathway that produced §6.5's proposal. The card must
   close it in the same sentence.
3. **The part's own protection permits more.** ILIM is 2.8 A typ (`:2021`), and the same AMR block rates
   "Duration of OUT_ Short Circuit to GND or VDD … Continuous" and "Duration of OUTP Short to OUTN … Continuous"
   (`:173-174`). The datasheet therefore contemplates fault currents above the number being installed as the
   never-exceed, which is further reason it is not a statement about what the branch will do.

**Required change.** Wherever ±1.6 A appears, append verbatim:

> **±1.6 A is a damage threshold, not an allowance and not a setting.** It is not a rail-sizing term, must never be
> entered in a `P(N)` or `Σ P(k)` cell, and must never be dialled into the bench PSU as the amp branch's current
> limit — T11's policy is the **lowest defensible limit per substep**, and the amplifier cannot draw anywhere near
> 1.6 A into a 4 Ω or 8 Ω speaker at 5 V. Its only use is negative: **no** figure, setting or allowance on this card
> may permit sustained amp-branch current above it.

### B7 — "the only three the part publishes" is false, and it drops the one M-PEAK-clean current maximum S4 could actually use

**Claim.** §6.1's card text: "**Manufacturer MAXIMUM (M-PEAK-clean), and the only three the part publishes:** IDD
3.35 mA max …, ±1.6 A …, and gain 8.4/9/9.6 dB". (§2.5 says four, listing abs-max dissipation as well — the report
contradicts itself.)

**Evidence.** The Electrical Characteristics table publishes several further guaranteed maxima, including two that are
**currents** and are directly relevant to this row:

- **Shutdown current ISHDN — 2 µA max** (`SD_MODE = 0 V`, TA = +25 °C, `:204`).
- **Standby current ISTNDBY — 400 µA max** (`SD_MODE = 1.8 V`, **no BCLK**, TA = +25 °C, `:205`).

Also guaranteed maxima: UVLO 2.3 V (`:199`), VOS ±2.5 mV (`:207`), THD+N 0.06 % (`:249`), frequency response +0.2 dB
(`:273`). The exhaustiveness claim is simply false, and it goes onto a card.

**Why it matters, not just a nit.** BG-03 S4 adds the amplifier to the rail. At the moment the load is added, the
plausible states are (a) powered with I²S clocks running and digital silence, and (b) powered with **no BCLK** — for
which the manufacturer publishes a **guaranteed maximum of 400 µA**, M-PEAK-clean, at the exact condition. That is a
genuinely usable number for the un-driven half of S4 and the report reached past it for a 1.6 A stress rating.

**Required change.** Strike "and the only three the part publishes". Add:

> Also M-PEAK-clean and directly applicable to this substep: **ISTNDBY 400 µA max** (`SD_MODE` high, **no BCLK**,
> TA = +25 °C, `:205`) and **ISHDN 2 µA max** (`SD_MODE` low, `:204`). If the amp is added to the rail with the I²S
> clocks not running, 400 µA max is the manufacturer maximum for that state. It bounds **only** that state — it is
> not a bound on the driven amp, which stays BLOCKED.

## NON-BLOCKING

### N1 — "the output current cannot exceed 1.375 A" asserts a hard limit from a formally BLOCKED impedance
§6.1 and §6.3: "into 4 Ω at ≤ 5.5 V the output current **cannot exceed 1.375 A**". 4 Ω is nominal, and DR2-8 has the
impedance **BLOCKED** (packet item 6); a nominal-4 Ω driver's impedance **minimum** is lower than its nominal, and the
datasheet's own test load is R + L (`:388-389`). At a 3.2 Ω minimum, 5.5 V gives 1.72 A — **above** the ±1.6 A the
same sentence installs as the never-exceed for `OUT_`. The conclusion (ILIM is short/fault protection only) survives
comfortably, but the "cannot" does not.
**Required change:** "into a purely resistive 4 Ω at ≤ 5.5 V the output current would not exceed ≈ 1.375 A; the fitted
speaker's impedance **minimum** is unverified (DR2-8, packet item 6), so treat this as indicative — ILIM is
short/fault protection, not a normal-operation bound."

### N2 — "Nothing in the shipped configuration clips" (§3.1) is unsupported and reassuring in the wrong direction
See B4 item 4. Not in §6 text, so non-blocking, but it must come out of the report body.
**Required change:** "At volume 100 the synth's **peaks** ride into the amplifier's clipping region (predicted peak
4.65 V against the ≈ 4.46 V the datasheet's `POUT` rows imply is reachable into 4 Ω). Because the synth's crest factor
is ≈ 3.2, this affects peaks only and does not change the RMS-derived power figures materially — but 'nothing clips'
is not true and must not be claimed."

### N3 — §6.4 step 7 (diode mode) is outside the datasheet's pin-voltage absolute maximum with the board unpowered
With VDD at 0 V, the AMR "All Other Pins to GND … −0.3 V to (VDD + 0.3 V)" (`:170`) makes **any** probe voltage above
+0.3 V on `GAIN_SLOT` an out-of-AMR condition. A DMM's diode source (~1 mA, ~2–3 V open circuit) stays well inside the
"Continuous Input Current (all other pins) ±20 mA" AMR (`:172`), so damage is unlikely and this is standard practice —
but a checklist that is otherwise scrupulous about AMRs must say so. Step 7's second direction (probe + on VDD) also
partially energises the board through the meter and, with bulk capacitance present, reads as a rising voltage rather
than a diode drop, so its diagnostic value beyond step 6 is small.
**Required change:** make step 7 **optional**, and add: "Use only a meter whose diode-test source is current-limited
(≈ 1 mA). Note that with the board unpowered this momentarily places the pin outside the datasheet's −0.3 V to
(VDD + 0.3 V) rating (`:170`), though well inside its ±20 mA input-current rating (`:172`); on a board with bulk
capacitance the VDD-side reading may rise slowly rather than settle, which is not a fault."

### N4 — §6.4 step 6 drops C6's discriminator qualifier

"≈ 100 kΩ to GND ⇒ 15 dB". With the report's own inferred ≈ 200 kΩ internal bias, a genuine 9 dB board also reads in
the 10⁵ Ω decade, so the shortened rule can misclassify a correct board.
**Required change:** restore "…and **clearly lower than the reading to the other rail**" to step 6's 15 dB and 3 dB
discriminators.

### N7 — the BTL / differential reading of the gain equation is asserted, not demonstrated — and the datasheet proves it
§2.2's A3 ("the bridge-tied output cannot swing beyond the rails") is called "physics, **not** a datasheet number".

### N8 — §8 item 8's "crest factor NOT DERIVABLE" is wrong, and that refusal is what let B2 stand
The RMS of a sum of sines at distinct harmonics is `sqrt(Σaᵢ²/2)`, phase-independent, and every amplitude is a
compile-time constant (`EngineSynth.hpp`:87-90, `SynthProfiles.hpp`:55-69, `EngineSynth.cpp`:127-137). The only
genuine ambiguity is the whine, which lands on an existing harmonic in both voices — and taking it coherently gives a
clean upper bound. This is the single item the report should not have refused.

**+14.8 % / −12.9 %** in power. Asymmetric, and the quoted 7.4 % matches neither edge.
